44clegacy feed-in tariff

Still on the old 44c solar feed-in tariff?

Many long-time Adelaide Hills homeowners are, and it's one of the most valuable things you can have. The SA scheme runs until 30 June 2028 for eligible systems.

Important: upgrading or enlarging your system can mean losing your 44c rate for good. Before you change anything, it's worth understanding exactly what you'd be giving up. That's exactly what a Health Check is for.
